Your page, set in stone

Mark the blocks of a page with data-wall. The page measures them, and the wall is cut around them in your browser.

Measured

Each block's box becomes a plate of dark glass in a gold rim.

Cut again

When the page reflows to a new width, the wall is cut again around its new layout.

Copy it

Copy wall.js and this script next to your page, with the engine/ directory, and change the import paths to match.
